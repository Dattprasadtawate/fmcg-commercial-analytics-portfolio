Hello! I'm **Dattprasad Tawate**, an MBA graduate specializing in Finance and Business Intelligence based in Berlin, Germany. I bridge the gap between operations and commercial strategy using data analytics.

This repository showcases an end-to-end commercial data analytics project analyzing sales performance, customer retention cohorts, channel economics, and SKU rationalization.

---

## 📂 Project Assets
* **Excel Dynamic Dashboard (`Whey_Protein_Sales_Analysis_2024_Interactive.xlsx`)**: Formula-driven executive dashboard featuring dynamic dropdown parameter filters (`SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`), KPI summary cards, and channel/category performance breakdowns.
* **SQL Commercial Analysis (`SQL Dattprasad.pdf`)**: 18 business queries executed in MySQL covering cohort retention, revenue attribution, channel AOV variance, and bottom-SKU risk identification.
* **Power BI Report (`Whey Protein sales in 2025 June '.pbix`)**: Executive report visualizing regional volumes, category revenue shares, and sales margins.

---

## 🔑 Key Findings & Business Insights
* **Revenue Quality**: Total gross revenue reached ₹94.8L, but ₹9.8L (10.4%) was tied up in cancelled, refunded, or returned orders, highlighting fulfillment friction.
* **Retention Deficit**: A 94.1% one-time buyer rate indicated an acquisition-heavy model with high churn, supporting the business case for an automated post-purchase replenishment trigger.
* **Channel Performance**: The Gym Pro Shop channel delivered the highest Average Order Value (AOV), while the End User (D2C) channel drove the highest baseline volume and gross margin.
* **Hero SKU Concentration**: Gainer 2KG drove over 29% of net revenue, functioning as the primary volume driver across urban hubs.
